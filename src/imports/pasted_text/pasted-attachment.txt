Design and build a complete, polished, responsive website for **Nebula**, an early-stage deep-tech project focused on enabling humanity to utilize resources beyond Earth and ultimately expand human presence beyond Earth.

## CORE IDENTITY

Nebula is the overarching project/organization.

**Helix** is an autonomous robotic system currently being researched and designed as part of Nebula.

Do NOT present Helix as a separate company.

The current work is focused primarily on extraterrestrial resources and the development of autonomous robotic systems capable of operating in difficult off-Earth environments.

Nebula's long-term objective is much larger: enabling the infrastructure and resource systems required for humanity to expand beyond Earth.

The website is primarily an **investor/researcher/founder credibility website**, not an e-commerce site, SaaS site, product marketplace, or sales funnel.

The site should communicate ambition, seriousness, technical curiosity, honesty, and long-term thinking.

Do not make unsupported claims about technology being completed or operational.

---

# VISUAL DIRECTION

Create a visual identity that feels like:

**frontier engineering + aerospace research + advanced robotics + premium modern technology.**

Avoid the generic "space startup" aesthetic.

Do NOT fill the website with rockets, astronauts, planets, stars, galaxies, emoji, excessive particle effects, glowing sci-fi interfaces, or decorative space graphics.

No rocket emoji.
No astronaut imagery.
No excessive stars.
No cheesy futuristic HUDs.
No excessive neon.
No cyberpunk aesthetic.

The website should feel technologically advanced because of its **typography, layout, imagery, precision, and interaction design**, not because of visual gimmicks.

Use a restrained dark visual system with strong contrast, sophisticated typography, generous whitespace, subtle technical details, and carefully selected imagery.

The homepage should be visually impressive and clearly above-average, but not overloaded.

Use approximately 5–7 meaningful visual moments throughout the entire site rather than placing images everywhere.

---

The homepage should be the strongest and most visually attractive page.

# DESIGN PRINCIPLES

The website must feel:

**real**
**ambitious**
**technical**
**young but serious**
**research-driven**
**honest**
**forward-looking**

It must NOT feel:

**corporate**
**salesy**
**overhyped**
**fake futuristic**
**generic space-themed**
**AI-generated startup template**

The most important design principle:

> **The visual ambition can be ahead of the current technology, but the written claims must never be ahead of reality.**

Use elegant empty states for unfinished areas rather than fake content.

---

# RESPONSIVENESS

Design desktop first but make the entire website fully responsive.

Support:

* desktop
* tablet
* mobile

Maintain typography hierarchy and image quality across breakpoints.

Navigation should become a clean mobile menu while retaining subtle animation.

---

# MICRO-INTERACTIONS

Use subtle interactions throughout:

* navigation hover animations
* active section indicators
* image reveal transitions
* card hover elevation/movement
* smooth scrolling
* subtle text reveals
* understated section transitions

Avoid:

* particle backgrounds
* cursor trails
* excessive parallax
* constant motion
* glowing borders everywhere
* loading screens
* unnecessary 3D effects

Motion should reinforce the interface, not become the interface.

---

# FINAL IMPRESSION

When someone lands on the website, the immediate impression should be:

**"These people are seriously thinking about how autonomous systems could enable resource utilization beyond Earth."**

After exploring it, the second impression should be:

**"They are early, but they are actually thinking through the hard problems instead of pretending they have already solved them."**

And after reaching Vision:

**"The robot is only one component of a much larger objective."**

The final site should look like the beginning of a serious frontier-technology organization—not a finished corporation pretending to already have a Moon factory.
